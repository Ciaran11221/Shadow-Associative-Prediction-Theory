# C08. Satellite methane alerts mostly go unanswered

**What happened.** Since 2022, UNEP's Methane Alert and Response System has notified governments and companies when satellites see a large methane leak. By October 2025 it had sent more than 3,500 alerts; replies rose from 1% to 12% in a year.

**What it shows.** Detection works at planetary scale, and the gaps get flagged (L3). Most alerts still have no one acting on them (R9).
