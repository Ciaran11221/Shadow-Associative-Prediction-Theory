# C07. Surfside: the warning that was read and put off

**What happened.** Satellite radar showed the Champlain Towers South area in Surfside, Florida sinking about 2 mm a year in 1993–1999; a geophysicist published it in 2020, in a study about flooding. He has said sinking alone likely wouldn't bring a building down. A 2018 engineer's report had already warned of major structural damage, and the main concrete repairs hadn't started when the building collapsed on 24 June 2021.

**What it shows.** In hindsight the weak shadow, sinking, looks prophetic, while the strong warning was read and put off. Flagging wasn't the gap; acting was (R9).
