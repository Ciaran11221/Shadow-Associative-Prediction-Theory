# C04. Volkswagen's diesels pass the test, fail the road

**What happened.** Volkswagen diesel cars detected when they were on an official test and ran clean only then. On the road they emitted up to 40 times the US limit for nitrogen oxides, a lung-damaging exhaust gas. Researchers at West Virginia University caught it in 2014 by measuring on real roads; the US Environmental Protection Agency announced the violation in September 2015.

**What it shows.** Every official test used the same procedure, so all those passes were one angle, copied (R2). An independent angle, on the road, found the truth (L2). It's also why a well-known shadow can be gamed ([THEORY.md](../THEORY.md), section 8).
