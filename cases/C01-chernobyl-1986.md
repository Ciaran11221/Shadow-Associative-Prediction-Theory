# C01. Chernobyl fallout found in Sweden before Moscow spoke

**What happened.** Reactor 4 at Chernobyl exploded on 26 April 1986. On 28 April, radiation monitors at Sweden's Forsmark nuclear plant picked up contamination that turned out to come from outside the plant, carried from the east. The Soviet Union had announced nothing; a brief statement followed that evening.

**What it shows.** The official angle was silent, and an independent angle in another country measured the shadow anyway (L2). The gap between silence and fallout was the signal (L3).
