# C16. A warning dismissed as rumour

**What happened.** On 30 December 2019, Wuhan doctor Li Wenliang warned colleagues in a private chat group about a SARS-like illness. On 3 January 2020, police made him sign a statement that he had spread false rumours. He died of COVID-19 on 7 February 2020.

**What it shows.** When it arrived, the warning was unverified and officially denied. A rule that deletes unverifiable inputs would have deleted this one, so they're parked instead (R4).
