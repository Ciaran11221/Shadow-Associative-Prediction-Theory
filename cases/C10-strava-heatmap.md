# C10. A fitness map exposes military bases

**What happened.** In January 2018, analysts noticed that Strava's public global heatmap, built from users' recorded runs and rides, showed the locations and layouts of military bases, including in Syria and Afghanistan.

**What it shows.** Each run was harmless; joined, they exposed places meant to stay hidden. This is why v0.1 holds no personal data (R6).
