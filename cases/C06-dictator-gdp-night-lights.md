# C06. Dictators' GDP checked against night-time light

**What happened.** Luis Martinez compared reported GDP growth with night-time light seen by satellites for 184 countries, 1992–2013. Autocracies overstate their yearly growth by about 35%: a real 2% reported as 2.7% (2% × 1.35). Journal of Political Economy, 2022.

**What it shows.** A shadow, light, exposed a gap in official numbers (L1, L3). It worked because countries with trusted figures set the reference for how light tracks growth, not the autocracies themselves (R3).
