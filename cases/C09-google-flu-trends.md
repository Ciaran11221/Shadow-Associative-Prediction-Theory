# C09. Google Flu Trends drifts off course

**What happened.** Google Flu Trends estimated flu levels from search queries. In February 2013 it estimated about double the level reported by the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Changes in how people searched, and in Google's own search, had moved the shadow, and it wasn't kept in check against official counts.

**What it shows.** A shadow that works at first can drift. L1 needs shadows that still hold on new data (the PROVEN line of the [shadow test](../docs/shadow-test.md)); this one is kept on record as [S12](../shadows/S12-flu-searches.md).
