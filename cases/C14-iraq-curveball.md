# C14. Iraq's mobile weapons labs: one source, many echoes

**What happened.** The US case that Iraq had mobile biological-weapons labs, presented at the UN in February 2003, rested mainly on one informant, codenamed Curveball, later judged a fabricator. Reports that seemed to back him up didn't hold up. In 2005 the US commission on Iraq intelligence found the pre-war judgements on Iraq's weapons "dead wrong".

**What it shows.** Echoes of one source look like agreement. Copies count once (R2); only independent angles add trust (L2).
