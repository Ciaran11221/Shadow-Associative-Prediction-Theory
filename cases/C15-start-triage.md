# C15. Triage cut-offs are chosen by people

**What happened.** Paramedics at mass-casualty scenes sort patients with START, a yes/no decision tree developed in California in 1983. Its cut-offs are numbers people chose: a patient breathing faster than 30 times a minute goes in the "immediate" group.

**What it shows.** Crisis decisions do break down into yes/no questions, but any question with a number in it needs a cut-off, and a person sets it (R7).
