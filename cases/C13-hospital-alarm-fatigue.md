# C13. Hospital alarms that staff learn to ignore

**What happened.** Reviews citing Sendelbach & Funk (2013) put the share of patient-monitor alarms that are false or need no action at 72% to 99%. Staff exposed to constant alarms learn to tune them out; this is called alarm fatigue.

**What it shows.** More eyes without filtering is noise. The method is judged by how few things it puts in front of a person (R8).
