# C05. China's coal figures revised up by as much as 17%

**What happened.** In November 2015, China's National Bureau of Statistics revised its own past coal figures up by as much as 17%: about 600 million tonnes more for 2012 alone, more than 70% of what the US burns in a year. Three years earlier, Guan et al. (2012) had found that China's national energy statistics and the sum of its provinces' statistics disagreed by 1.4 billion tonnes of CO2 for 2010, roughly Japan's annual output.

**What it shows.** A verified, dated official source was wrong for years (R1). The gap between two official numbers that should have matched flagged it early (L3). Any shadow calibrated on China's own figures would have inherited the error (R3).
