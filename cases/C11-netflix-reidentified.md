# C11. "Anonymous" Netflix ratings re-identified

**What happened.** In 2006 Netflix released about 100 million movie ratings from roughly 480,000 customers, with names removed, for a public contest. Arvind Narayanan and Vitaly Shmatikov showed that people could be identified by matching their ratings with public reviews on IMDb. Netflix cancelled a planned second contest in 2010.

**What it shows.** Cross-checking public data is also how people get unmasked. Accuracy isn't safety (R6).
