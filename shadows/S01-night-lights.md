# S01. Night-time light for economic activity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | Real economic activity, where official GDP may be bent |
| Shadow | Night-time light measured by satellites |
| Why it moves | More activity means more electric light |
| Other causes | Electrification drives, gas flares, fires, cloud, moonlight |
| Calibrated on | Countries with trusted statistics, not the one being checked (R3) |
| Speed | Yearly |
| Record | 1992–2013 from older satellites; a newer series from 2012 |

**Known result.** Across 184 countries, 1992–2013, autocracies overstate yearly GDP growth by about 35% ([C06](../cases/C06-dictator-gdp-night-lights.md)).
