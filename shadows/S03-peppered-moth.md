# S03. Peppered moth colour for soot

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | Soot pollution over decades |
| Shadow | The share of dark peppered moths |
| Why it moves | Soot blackened tree bark and killed pale lichens, so dark moths were better hidden from birds |
| Other causes | Changes in bird predation; moths moving between areas |
| Calibrated on | Not formally: it tracks the direction of pollution, not the amount |
| Speed | Generations |
| Record | First dark moth recorded in Manchester in 1848; dark forms declined after the 1956 Clean Air Act |
