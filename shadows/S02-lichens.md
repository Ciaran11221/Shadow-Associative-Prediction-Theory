# S02. Lichens for sulphur dioxide

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | Sulphur dioxide levels, especially where nobody measured them |
| Shadow | Which lichen species grow on trees and walls |
| Why it moves | Lichens take what they need from air and rain, and sulphur dioxide kills the sensitive species |
| Other causes | Nitrogen pollution, climate, bark type, habitat |
| Calibrated on | Measured sulphur dioxide: the 1970 lichen scale for England and Wales was matched to measured winter levels |
| Speed | Years |
| Record | Field surveys and museum collections |
