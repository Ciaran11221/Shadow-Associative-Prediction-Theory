# S04. Ship tracks for sulphur in ship exhaust

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | Sulphur pollution from ships at sea |
| Shadow | Bright lines of cloud seeded by ship exhaust |
| Why it moves | Sulphur particles seed extra cloud droplets, which makes the cloud brighter |
| Other causes | They only form in some low-cloud conditions; ship traffic changes |
| Calibrated on | Shipping traffic and fuel rules |
| Speed | Hours |
| Record | Described from satellite images since the 1960s; an AI-built series from 2003 |

**Known result.** NASA researchers used AI to find ship tracks in satellite images from 2003 to 2020. They fell to record lows after a 2020 rule cut sulphur in ship fuel from 3.5% to 0.5%, and dipped after the 2008 financial crisis.
