# S05. Cooling-tower steam for power plant output

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | How hard a power plant is running, and so its CO2 |
| Shadow | Visible water-vapour plumes from cooling towers and some flue stacks |
| Why it moves | A running plant makes steam; more output, bigger plumes |
| Other causes | Weather (humidity, temperature, wind) and plant design |
| Calibrated on | Reported generation in the US, Europe and Australia |
| Speed | Each satellite pass |
| Limits | Only plants with wet or mechanical-draft cooling towers, or wet flue-gas desulphurisation; applied to plants making 32% of global power-plant CO2 |

This is the method Climate TRACE uses for power plants.
