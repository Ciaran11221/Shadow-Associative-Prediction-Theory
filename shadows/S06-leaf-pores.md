# S06. Leaf pores for past CO2

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | CO2 levels in the past |
| Shadow | The number of stomata (breathing pores) per area of leaf |
| Why it moves | Many plants grow fewer pores when CO2 is higher |
| Other causes | Species, light, water |
| Calibrated on | CO2 records from ice cores |
| Speed | Decades |
| Record | Museum (herbarium) leaves, and fossils |

**Known result.** Museum leaves of 8 tree species collected over 200 years show 40% fewer pores (Woodward, Nature, 1987). Later work found the response varies by species.

## Sources

- [Woodward 1987, Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/327617a0)
