# S08. Bird counts for ozone pollution

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | Harm from ground-level ozone |
| Shadow | Bird numbers from eBird, a citizen-science count |
| Why it moves | Ozone harms birds directly and through the plants and insects they eat |
| Other causes | Habitat loss, pesticides, cats, climate |
| Calibrated on | Measured ozone, and a pollution rule that changed it (the NOx Budget Trading Program) |
| Speed | Seasons to years |
| Record | eBird data; this study covered 3,214 US counties over 15 years |

**Known result.** The authors estimate that cuts in ozone kept about 1.5 billion birds alive (Liang et al., PNAS, 2020). The figure is scaled up from relative counts, not counted directly.
