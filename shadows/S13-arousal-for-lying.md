# S13. Bodily arousal for lying (failed)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | Whether someone is lying |
| Shadow | Bodily arousal: pulse, breathing, skin |
| What happened | The US National Academies found the polygraph too inaccurate for security screening ([C12](../cases/C12-polygraph-screening.md)) |
| Why it failed | Fear and anxiety move the same signals; screening many people produces many false positives for each true one |
