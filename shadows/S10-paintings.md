# S10. Paintings for 19th-century haze

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Hidden quantity | Air pollution before instruments |
| Shadow | Contrast and colour in paintings |
| Why it moves | Haze lowers contrast and whitens the light painters saw |
| Other causes | Artistic style |
| Calibrated on | Historical pollution estimates for London and Paris |
| Speed | Not applicable |
| Record | 1790s to 1900s |

**Known result.** 60 Turner and 38 Monet paintings got hazier and whiter as coal smoke rose; pollution explains part of the change, not all of it (Albright & Huybers, PNAS, 2023).
